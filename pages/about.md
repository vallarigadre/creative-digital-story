Hi, I'm V. I spent a decade in retail merchandise planning, which is a polite way of saying I have built a lot of forecasts, spreadsheets and buying plans for other people. These days I also run Creative Digital Story, a small digital product studio, on the side.

Here is the part that makes this blog different: almost everything I make is built with AI. My blogs research and draft their own posts on a schedule. My interactive guides, trackers and courses are built with AI from a blank page. I still decide what to make, edit every word and test every link, but AI does a remarkable share of the heavy lifting.

## What you'll find here

This blog is my build log. Every Saturday I publish one entry on how a real piece of my business works:

- **Behind the Build:** how each blog, tool and product actually got made, prompts included.
- **AI Tools:** plain-English walkthroughs of the tools I use, for people who don't code.
- **Income Experiments:** what I tried, what it cost and what it earned, flops included.
- **Playbooks:** step-by-step plans for Etsy, Pinterest, Threads, print on demand and digital products.

## What you won't find

No "make $10k in a weekend" promises. Building a business with AI is real, but it is still a business: it takes testing, patience and a lot of small fixes. I would rather show you the honest middle of the process than a highlight reel.

## How this blog is made

Fittingly, this blog is partly automated too. A scheduled AI task researches and drafts each week's entry, then I review it, add my own screenshots and numbers, and publish. You can read exactly how that works in the build log.
