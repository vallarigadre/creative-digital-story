This site keeps data collection to a minimum.

## Newsletter

If you join The Saturday Build, your email address is stored by the site's host, Netlify, and used only to send you the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

## Analytics

The site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.

## Links to other sites

Links to my shop and to other tools take you to their own websites, which have their own privacy policies. Affiliate links may let those sites know you arrived from here.

## Questions

You can reach me through the Creative Digital Story shop page if you have any questions about your data.
