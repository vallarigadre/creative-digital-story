# Creative Digital Story: photo style

## Mix the photos (V, 9 Nov 2026)

Not every photo shows the woman. Mix two kinds so the blog doesn't look repetitive:

- People photos: the CDS mom (and sometimes her toddler) as described below.
- Styled still-life photos with no people, in the same look as the shop images: a warm desk or table scene with a tablet or laptop showing soft blush planner or pin designs, a linen pin board of photos, kraft gift parcels with blush ribbon, notebooks, a ribbed mug, dried flowers and gypsophila, gold clips, warm golden light, cream, blush and warm wood tones.

Per post: 2 people photos and 2 still-life photos. Covers alternate week to week: if the newest published cover shows the woman, the next cover is a still life, and the other way round.

Every blog photo shows the same woman (the CDS mom) and, in some photos, her toddler daughter. They are made in ChatGPT. Keep all images for one post in one ChatGPT chat. If you can, attach `brand/cds-woman-reference.jpg` (the home page photo) at the start of a new chat as the look to match. She was first made on 9 Nov 2026 from V's Pinterest reference of a mom in a mirror selfie holding her baby.

## Paste this first

I'm making a set of photos for my blog. Use the attached reference image if there is one, and keep the same woman in every image: a slim woman in her early 30s with fair to light skin and long, loose, straight to softly wavy medium-brown hair with a center part, falling well past her shoulders, never tied up in a bun. She has slender hands with almond nails in a milky pale pink polish and a thin gold ring, and often wears an oversized oatmeal or cream ribbed knit sweater with very wide sleeves. Hide her face as much as possible in every image: crop the frame above her lips or at her shoulders, turn her away, have her look down so only her hair shows, or hide her face behind a laptop she holds up or behind her phone in a mirror selfie. Never show a full, clear face. In some images her toddler daughter is with her: about 18 months old in November 2026 and growing a little older each month, in soft taupe knitwear (a taupe ribbed beanie, a knit cardigan, knit booties). Only her hands, feet, legs, back or the back of her head show, never her face. The look: candid, real photos that look like she took them on her phone at home, the kind of cozy "mom working from home" shots saved on Pinterest, not polished AI renders or stock photos. She wears soft cream or oatmeal knit loungewear (chunky ribbed sweaters with wide sleeves, knit lounge pants, fuzzy shearling slippers). Settings: a textured oat or greige sofa, a bed with white sheets, a small round white table, plain white walls and sheer linen curtains. Palette: cream, ivory, oat, greige, soft taupe and warm beige. Light: soft, diffused daylight from the window, gentle natural shadows. Realism: shot on an iPhone, natural phone-camera look, slightly imperfect candid framing, real skin texture, visible knit texture, slight grain, a lived-in home, muted true-to-life colors, no glossy sheen, no studio lighting, no heavy blur. No readable text, logos or brand marks anywhere: laptops are plain silver with no logo, screens show only soft blurred content. Every image should look like a real, unedited phone photo, not an AI image or a stock photo.
